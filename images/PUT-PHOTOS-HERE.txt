PROVENANCE IS NOT ESTABLISHED.

The uploads are third-party web images of unknown origin, supplied for this fan archive.
No licence is claimed on the page and none should be claimed in the footer. If the site
goes public, either get permission for these four or swap in free-licensed photographs
(Wikimedia Commons has several of Roumie under CC BY-SA) and credit them properly.

They are also low resolution — the largest source is 473 px on the long edge — so they
have been upscaled to their display size and look soft next to text at large widths.
If you replace them, prefer sources of 1200 px or more on the long edge.

To swap a photograph: drop the new file in pic/, crop it to the matching shape above,
update the alt text and the About caption, and change the ?v= number on the <img> tags
so browsers fetch the new file instead of a cached copy.

Format: JPEG or PNG. HEIC from an iPhone needs converting first (Photos > Export, or
`heif-convert`), since browsers will not display it. WebP works in every current browser.
