All four are crops of photographs by Patrick.suechan, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia
Commons. Attribution lives in ../CREDITS.md and in the footer of the page — keep it
there; the licence requires it.

To replace a photograph, drop the new file in ../images/incoming/ and it will be cropped
to the matching shape. Rendering is at up to ~700 CSS px wide, so anything below
1200 px on the long edge will look soft on high-DPI screens.
