TO SWAP A PHOTOGRAPH
--------------------
1. Get a source at least 1200 px on the long edge, ideally more.
2. Crop to the shape in the table above, scale down (never up), save as progressive JPEG.
3. Update the alt text, the About caption, the press-frame credit line and the footer
   credit paragraph together.
4. Bump the ?v= number on the <img> tags so browsers fetch the new file.

Only use images you may publish: your own, free-licensed ones (Commons, Flickr CC) with the
photographer and licence recorded, or press frames you have written permission for.

Format: JPEG or PNG. HEIC from an iPhone must be converted first. WebP works in every
current browser.
