## Modifications

All four were cropped to the layout's aspect ratios, upscaled with Lanczos resampling, given a light
unsharp mask and a 4% saturation lift, then saved as progressive JPEG at quality 88. The upscaling is
substantial — the sources are web thumbnails, 180–473 px on the long edge — so the images are soft
compared with photographs of print resolution. The output dimensions are matched to what the layout
actually displays, not to the original 1000 × 1250 slots.

## Not used

Five uploads were left out:

- `download (2).webp`, `download (3).webp` — *The Chosen* key art with the show's title lettering
  across the frame, which reads as promotional artwork rather than a photograph.
- `th.webp` — a frame with a cross drawn across the forehead.
- `th (1).webp`, `th (2).webp` — 180 × 180 squares, too small to fill any slot.
