## Modifications

- `hero.jpg` — 4:5 crop from the 1280 × 1921 file, resized to 1000 × 1250.
- `about.jpg` — 4:5 crop of Roumie from the 1280 × 1828 frame, resized to 720 × 900.
- `press-02.jpg` — square crop from the 1280 × 1001 still, resized to 900 × 900.
- `press-03.jpg` — 3:2 crop from the 1280 × 720 still, resized to 1200 × 800.

Each was resized down (never up) with Lanczos resampling and a light unsharp mask, then saved as
progressive JPEG at quality 88. Under CC BY-SA 4.0 a crop is an adaptation, so these files stay under
the same licence; copyright in the originals stays with the photographer or studio.

## Superseded: the uploaded thumbnails

`images/pic/` still holds nine images uploaded earlier. Four of them were used for a while, but they
are web thumbnails — 180–473 px on the long edge — and looked soft once scaled up, so they were
replaced by the files above. No higher-resolution free-licensed version of those four specific
pictures exists: they are promotional artwork, a broadcaster's interview frame and a magazine-style
portrait, all rights-reserved. The uploads are kept for reference and are not used by the page.

## Higher-resolution sources that exist

- **The Chosen press centre** (`press.thechosen.tv`) publishes a Jonathan Roumie headshot at
  4520 × 5308 and behind-the-scenes stills up to 6240 × 4160, under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms the
  Commons files cite. They sit behind a JavaScript asset app, so they need to be downloaded by hand.
- Commons itself carries stills up to 6000 px wide; the four above were taken at 1280–1920 px, which
  covers the layout's display size with room for high-DPI screens.
